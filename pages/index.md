## Start here

This blog was scaffolded from the Nera blog starter. The newest posts are
listed below, the archive has all of them, and every tag has a page of its own.

## What to edit first

1. **Your address:** replace `origin: https://example.com` in
   `config/app.yaml` with your site's real address before you deploy. The
   feed and the sitemap are built from it.
2. **The name:** `name` in `config/app.yaml`, shown in the header, the
   page titles and the feed.
3. **The legal pages:** the [imprint](/imprint.html) and the
   [privacy policy](/privacy.html) are placeholders. Replace them with your
   own texts before you publish.
4. **The sample posts:** read them, then replace them with your own in
   `pages/posts/`. Also replace the social link in
   `config/social-media-links.yaml` and the [about](/about.html) page.

Run `nera dev` to preview the blog while you edit.
